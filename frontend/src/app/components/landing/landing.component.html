Student Workflow

How FocusFlow Elevates Your Academic Journey

A seamless 4-step workflow connecting syllabus tracking to verified retention.

01

Configure Your Courses

Add your subjects, set exam deadlines, and define syllabus topics with estimated duration and difficulty levels (Easy, Medium, Hard).

02

Generate Daily Plan

Specify your available study hours for the day. FocusFlow calculates priorities and allocates a realistic schedule with ML duration forecasts.

03

Evaluate Understanding

Take subject or topic quizzes. The evaluation engine automatically categorizes your topic mastery and flags weak concepts on your dashboard.

04

Retain & Review with SM-2

Review daily due flashcards with SuperMemo SM-2 spaced repetition. As you rate recall, the system calculates optimal review intervals for maximum retention.
